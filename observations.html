Curious by nature
A field guide to the cosmos

Space is full
of wonders.

Meet a few of the remarkable objects that make our universe so fascinating. Pick a subject and let your curiosity travel.

✦Stellar neighbors

Stars

Giant spheres of hot gas that make their own light. Our Sun is the closest star to Earth.

✺Island universes

Galaxies

Vast communities of stars, gas, and dust. Our home galaxy is the Milky Way.

◉Worlds

Planets

Worlds that orbit stars—from rocky Mercury to the giant planets of our solar system.

◌Orbiting companions

Moons

Natural companions that orbit planets. Saturn’s moon Titan has lakes of liquid methane.

☁Cosmic clouds

Nebulae

Enormous clouds of gas and dust, where stars can form and shape new worlds.

☄Icy travelers

Comets

Icy bodies that grow glowing comas and tails when they approach the Sun.

⟡Ancient leftovers

Asteroids

Rocky objects left over from our solar system’s formation, many found between Mars and Jupiter.

◎Extreme gravity

Black holes

Regions where gravity is so strong that nothing—not even light—can escape from beyond the event horizon.
